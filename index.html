Privacy Policy

How CarLarp collects, uses, and protects your information.

1Who we are

CarLarp (“the app,” “we,” “us”) is a car-spotting game operated by Raymond Calubayan. This policy explains what information the app handles when you use it. If you do not agree with it, please do not use CarLarp.

2Information we collect

  • Account information. You can use CarLarp as a guest with no account. If you choose to sign in, we use Sign in with Apple, which gives us an anonymised Apple user identifier and a relay email address. Apple's sign-in sheet may also offer to share your name; CarLarp does not store or use it. Guest sessions are identified only by an anonymous identifier created on your device.
  • Photos you capture or import. When you catch a car, the photo you take (or a photo you choose from your library to identify) is processed for recognition and, if the catch is saved, stored with your account.
  • Approximate location. If you grant location access, we record where a catch happened only as a deliberately blurred coordinate. Your position is snapped to an area of roughly a city block on your device before anything leaves the phone, and is never drawn on the map tighter than about 800 metres. We do not collect or store your precise location.
  • Gameplay data. The cars you catch, your score, level, quests, and who you follow.
  • Profile information. A username, an optional avatar photo, and an optional home city, if you set them.
  • Purchases. Purchases are processed by Apple. We receive confirmation that a purchase occurred and what it was for; we never receive your card or payment details.
  • Diagnostic and product data. Limited technical logs used to keep the app working, and a record of cars the app could identify but had no class for — the car's name, plus an anonymous identifier for your installation — which is how we decide which cars to add next. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

3How we use your information

  • To identify the cars you photograph and score your catches.
  • To run the game: your Garage, collection, levels, quests, and leaderboard rank.
  • To show catches on the Radar as an approximate heat map.
  • To operate social features you choose to use — the feed, following, and profiles.
  • To process purchases and provide membership benefits.
  • To keep the app secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.

4Car recognition and service providers

Identifying a car uses a combination of on-device processing and a cloud service. When cloud recognition is used, the car photo is sent to a third-party AI provider (Google) for the sole purpose of identifying the vehicle. We rely on the following providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — hosting, database, authentication, and photo storage.
  • Google (Gemini API) — cloud car identification from your photo.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple and in-app purchases. If you turn on “Save shots to Photos”, each catch is also written to your own camera roll, which your device may back up to your personal iCloud. That copy belongs to your Apple account, not to us.

We do not sell your personal information.

5What other players can see

CarLarp is social by design, but you control your exposure:

  • If your profile is public, your username, avatar, and catches can appear on the leaderboard, the feed, and the Radar. A catch’s photo is shown to others only where your username already is, and only when you have chosen to share that catch’s photo.
  • If your profile is private, your catches stay off other players’ Radar and your profile is not shown to others, regardless of other settings.
  • You can report or block other users, and hide content, at any time. Reported and blocked content is removed from your experience, and every social view filters blocked, hidden, and reported content.

6Location, in detail

Location is optional and used only to place catches on the Radar. The exact coordinate is never transmitted or stored: it is blurred on your device to about a city-block-sized area before it is saved, and the map never renders a catch more tightly than roughly 800 metres. You can turn location off in your device settings at any time; the app still works without it.

7Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from within the app (Settings → Account), which removes your profile, catches, and associated data from our systems. Some records may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

8Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with access controls that limit each account to its own data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

9Children

CarLarp is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will remove it.

10Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. You can exercise most of these directly in the app, or by contacting us at the address below. If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, our lawful bases for processing are performing our contract with you (running the game), your consent (location), and our legitimate interests (security and abuse prevention).

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date at the top, and where appropriate we will notify you in the app.
