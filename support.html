Common questions

The quick answers. If yours isn't here, just email us.

·How do I catch a car?

Point your camera at a car and take the shot. CarLarp identifies the make and model and adds it to your Garage. You can also import a photo of a car from your library to identify it.

·What is CarLarp+?

An optional membership that unlocks more renders and extra perks. You can play, catch, and climb the leaderboard for free. Manage or cancel a subscription anytime in your Apple Account settings.

·How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings → Account → Delete Account. This removes your profile, catches, and associated data.

·How do I report or block someone?

Open the profile or a post, tap the menu, and choose Report or Block. Blocked and reported content is removed from your experience right away.

·Is my location shared?

Only as an approximate area. Your position is blurred to about a city block on your device before anything is saved, and the map never shows a catch more tightly than roughly 800 metres. Location is optional — the app works without it.
